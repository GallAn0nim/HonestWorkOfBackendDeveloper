Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Short version

No tracking, no ads, no analytics, nothing loaded from other companies. One cookie, and only after you sign in. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Who is responsible

Oskar Gruszczyński, oskar171@gmail.com. Write to this address with any question about your data.

What is processed and why

  • Your IP address and request details (time, page, browser), because a web server cannot answer you without them. The hosting provider may keep them in access logs to run and protect the server. Legal basis: legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f). They are deleted when the hosting provider's log retention period ends.
  • What you type into the login hex. The username and password are checked against the demo accounts and are not stored. The demo accounts hold no real person's data.
  • Email you send me. If you write to me, I keep your message and address to reply, and delete them when the conversation is over. Legal basis: legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f).

The live logs in the observability hex are kept in memory only, never contain IP addresses or what you typed, and are gone when the server restarts.

Cookies

One cookie, .AspNetCore.Cookies, set only when you sign in to a demo account. It keeps you signed in, is not readable by scripts (HttpOnly), is never sent to other sites (SameSite=Strict), and is deleted when you sign out or close the browser. It is strictly necessary for the sign-in you asked for, so it needs no consent.

Who else sees the data

Only the hosting provider that runs the server, as a data processor bound by a data processing agreement. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing. The GitHub and LinkedIn links take you to those sites, whose own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or erasure, restriction of processing, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).
